# How Zelle can be easier on the invoice: the Zelle tag, a QR code, and a tap-to-pay link

Research for [#116](https://github.com/bigapejit/expand-handyman-RP/issues/116), part of the Pay now map [#109](https://github.com/bigapejit/expand-handyman-RP/issues/109). The paper today prints "Zelle: send to {zelleEmail}. The payment shows as Expand Handyman LLC." (`components/invoice-paper.tsx`, How to pay block), with the address read from the one `settings` row (`convex/settings.ts`, default `pay@expandhandyman.com` in `lib/expand-business.ts`). The owner's U.S. Bank business account is enrolled with Zelle under the **tag** `expandhandyman`, and its QR code decodes to `https://enroll.zellepay.com/qr-codes?data=` + base64 of `{"token":"expandhandyman","name":"EXPAND","action":"PAYMENT"}`.

Checked on 2026-09-23 against zelle.com (zellepay.com now redirects there), Early Warning's Zelle Network Standard Terms, the help pages of U.S. Bank, Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo and PNC, and the QR URL itself, fetched with curl and opened in a desktop browser. No bank was logged in to and no money was moved. Each claim is marked **verified** (a named page says it), **observed** (I saw it happen, with how), or **unverified** (neither).

## Short answer

1. **The amount cannot be preset.** No Zelle QR code, link or payload carries an amount, and U.S. Bank says so in plain words: "you can't add an amount when using a QR code. The recipient chooses how much to send." Adding `amount` or `memo` fields to the QR payload changes nothing the web page does; the bank apps that read the payload were not tested. The one Zelle feature that does put a set amount in front of the customer is a **request**, sent by the owner from the bank app to the customer's email or phone number. It carries the amount and a memo, but it works only if that email or number is already enrolled with Zelle, and nothing outside the bank app can create one: there is no API and no link. Zelle's small-business features include nothing that fixes an amount.
2. **A tag works like an email.** Customers type `expandhandyman` where they would type an email or phone number. Zelle, U.S. Bank, Bank of America, Chase, Wells Fargo and PNC all document sending to a tag. Nothing says every one of Zelle's 2,200+ banks supports it yet (Zelle says banks "are rolling out" tags). The tag can replace the email on the paper. The email is worth keeping only if it is enrolled with Zelle too. If `pay@expandhandyman.com` is not enrolled, the paper is sending customers to an address that cannot receive money.
3. **Tapping the QR URL does not open anyone's bank app.** It opens Zelle's "Find Your Bank" page on any device. The customer searches for their bank, and only for 7 large banks does the page offer a "Continue to your bank" link that carries the payee on to the bank. For about 1,960 banks it just says to open the banking app and scan the code, which a customer cannot do when the code is on the phone they are reading it on. No documented deep link exists, and the URL is not an app link on iOS or Android.
4. **Drawing the QR ourselves is technically sound.** The payload is plain base64 JSON with no signature, nonce or expiry, and re-encoding the same three fields reproduces the bank's string byte for byte. But the format is undocumented, so it can change. Bank of America explicitly suggests putting the QR code and tag on invoices. The "if you unenroll, shared codes will be invalid" warning appears only in the bank's app, not on any public page.
5. **The app should hold the tag.** It can also hold an optional email, but only one enrolled with Zelle. The paper should print the tag as text first, then the QR code under it for customers paying from a laptop or another phone. On the invoice link, a tap-to-pay button that lands on "Find Your Bank" is not worth building.
6. **For matching:** ask the customer to put the invoice number in the Zelle memo. Zelle and U.S. Bank document the memo, and U.S. Bank shows received memos under Activity. Whether every sending bank passes the memo through is unverified.

## 1. What a Zelle tag is and how a customer uses one

- **Verified (Zelle):** a tag is a small-business handle, "six to 40 characters. Letters, numbers, and hyphens … Tags aren't case sensitive"; businesses create one in their banking app and "Save it, share it and start getting paid"; only eligible small-business account holders can create one ([Zelle blog, Aug 15 2025](https://www.zelle.com/blog/small-businesses-get-their-own-handle-zelle-tag); [Zelle FAQ, tag](https://www.zelle.com/faq/i-was-asked-send-money-zelle-tag-small-business)).
- **Verified (Zelle), how the customer pays:** "Sending to a Zelle tag is just like sending to a recipient's email address or U.S. mobile number. Find Zelle in your mobile banking app, click 'Send,' and from the 'Select Recipient' screen, type the recipient's Zelle tag. Then, enter the amount, hit 'Send'", and check "the contact name that is displayed before sending money" ([Zelle FAQ, tag](https://www.zelle.com/faq/i-was-asked-send-money-zelle-tag-small-business)).
- **Verified (banks) that their customers can send to a tag:** U.S. Bank ("Select their email address, mobile number or Zelle tag (business accounts only)" in [KB0069382](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0069382.html); "A small business can register a Zelle tag for you to use to send them money" in [KB0070083](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0070083.html)); Bank of America ("Consumer clients can send money to small businesses enrolled with a Zelle tag"; the pay screen's search field is "Add/search name, tag, contact", [BofA Zelle](https://info.bankofamerica.com/en/digital-banking/zelle)); Wells Fargo (tags enrolled like an email or number, "only available for small business customers", [WF Zelle FAQs](https://www.wellsfargo.com/help/online-banking/zelle-faqs/)); Chase (tag rules and creation on its [business Zelle support page](https://www.chase.com/business/support/banking/online-banking/zelle)); PNC ("both business and consumer clients can send money to them", [PNC small business Zelle](https://www.pnc.com/en/small-business/banking/zelle.html)).
- **Unverified:** that *every* bank and credit union's Zelle screen accepts a tag. Zelle's own blog describes banks as "rolling out" the feature, and no page lists which banks support sending to one. A customer at a small credit union may find no way to enter it. Nothing I found says a tag is refused anywhere either.
- **Verified (Standard Terms):** the network's terms let the bank or Zelle "remove a User Zelle tag at any time and for any reason without notice" or "require you to change your Zelle tag" ([Zelle Network Standard Terms, effective Apr 30 2025, as hosted by a member bank](https://onlinelcsb.com/assets/files/rP5RQoXy)). So a tag is not guaranteed forever, which is one more reason to keep it in a setting rather than in code.
- **Tag instead of email.** The tag can stand on the paper by itself: Zelle's business page says customers can pay "using just your business email address, U.S. mobile number or Zelle tag" ([zelle.com/business](https://www.zelle.com/business)), and U.S. Bank sells the tag as the way to get paid "without providing your enrolled email address or U.S. mobile number" ([U.S. Bank, Mar 24 2026](https://www.usbank.com/about-us-bank/news-and-stories/article-library/customizing-the-zelle-experience-for-small-business-owners.html)). Keeping an email next to it helps only customers whose bank does not take tags, and only if that email is itself enrolled to the business account. **Unverified, and the owner must check:** whether `pay@expandhandyman.com` is enrolled with Zelle at all. The bank shows "Enrolled contact: expandhandyman". If money goes to an address nobody has enrolled, it waits 14 days for someone to enroll and then goes back to the customer ([Zelle FAQ, recipient not enrolled](https://www.zelle.com/faq/what-if-person-i-am-sending-money-hasnt-enrolled-zelle)).

## 2. What the QR URL does when opened

Everything below is **observed** unless marked otherwise: curl (`-sIL`, `-sL`, with desktop, iPhone and Android user agents), the page's own JavaScript, and a desktop browser in which I clicked banks in the list and read the resulting links.

- **The page.** `GET https://enroll.zellepay.com/qr-codes?data=…` returns 200, no redirect, a 1.2 MB Drupal page titled "Find Your Bank | Zelle Enroll" (Cloudflare, `Cache-Control: max-age=1800, public`). It opens with a modal: "Zelle QR Codes … Search for your bank … your contact's information is auto-filled to save you time. START SEARCH", then a searchable list of 2,583 banks. The payee's tag and name appear nowhere on the page.
- **What the script does with `data`.** The page's settings (`drupalSettings.findMyBank.scenarios`) define a `/qr-codes/` scenario keyed on the `data` parameter. The script (`js_3SUCQr…js`, "JS for Find My Bank page") never decodes the parameter. When the customer taps a bank, it appends `context=qr-codes&data=<the same base64>` to that bank's link, if the bank has one. It also sets a `selected_bank` cookie, so the next QR link the same browser opens jumps straight to that bank's box.
- **What the customer sees after picking a bank** (clicked in the browser, 2026-09-23):
  - **7 banks** (Bank of America, Capital One, Chase, PNC, Truist, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo) get "Great News! [bank] offers Zelle QR Codes. To continue sending money with Zelle QR Code, just log into your banking app" and a **Continue to your bank** link carrying `data`:
    - U.S. Bank: `https://usbankflyer.onelink.me/Woj5?…af_dp=usbank://zelleEnroll…&context=qr-codes&data=…`. On desktop it redirects to `usbank.com/online-mobile-banking/enroll-with-zelle.html?…&data=…`. With an iPhone user agent and no app it redirects to the App Store. With an Android user agent it serves an intent page opening `usbank://zelleEnroll?…&context=qr-codes&data=…`. The U.S. Bank iOS apps claim `/Woj5/*` on that host as a universal link. U.S. Bank documents the result: after "Continue to your bank" and login, "You'll be directed to the 'Send money' page where your recipient's details will populate. Complete the Enter Amount…" ([KB0213890](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0213890.html)).
    - Wells Fargo: `https://connect.secure.wellsfargo.com/auth/login/p2pAmount?context=qr-codes&data=…`. In a browser (desktop, iPhone and Android user agents) it returns a 302 to `wellsfargo.com/mobile-online-banking/apps/`, the app download page. The Wells Fargo iOS app claims `/auth/login/p2pAmount/*` as a universal link (from Wells Fargo's `apple-app-site-association`). The path name suggests an enter-amount screen, but the link carries only `context` and `data`, not an amount. **Unverified:** what the app shows.
    - Bank of America: goes on to `secure.bankofamerica.com/move-money/public/zelle-registration-login/?…&context=qr-codes&data=…` on desktop, and to a mobile interstitial carrying the same `data` on iPhone. So BofA carries the payee into its login flow. **Unverified:** what the logged-in screen shows.
    - Chase: `chase.com/personal/zelle/receive?…` redirects to the generic `chase.com/personal/zelle` marketing page, and the payee is dropped.
  - **About 1,960 banks** get "[bank] offers Zelle QR codes, simply scan in your banking app to pay … Close your browser, open and log into your banking app … tap the 'QR Code' icon to scan", with **no link at all**. BECU, a large Washington credit union, is one of them.
  - **About 616 banks** get "Zelle QR codes are coming soon … Continue using Zelle in your banking app" and a link to the bank's site, for example Banner Bank's `zelle.jhapaycenter.com/J2F.html?…&data=…`.
- **App links.** `enroll.zellepay.com/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` claims only `/mobile`, and only for Zelle's own apps (`com.zellepay.zelle`). `assetlinks.json` lists only those same Zelle apps. Zelle retired its standalone app ("there is not a standalone Zelle app", [zelle.com/blog/how-do-i-access-zelle](https://www.zelle.com/blog/how-do-i-access-zelle)), and no bank app is associated with the domain. So on iOS and Android, tapping the QR URL opens the browser page above, not a banking app. That follows from the association files; I did not test it on a device. No `zelle://` scheme or documented deep link exists.
- **Laptop.** The same bank finder. For the 7 banks, the link leads to the bank's website login, and whether the payee survives login there is unverified except for U.S. Bank's documented flow. For most banks it is a dead end.
- **Documented use.** Zelle documents the code only for scanning *inside* a banking app: "Find Zelle in your banking app, click 'Send,' then click on the QR code icon … point your camera at the recipient's Zelle QR code, enter the amount, hit 'Send'". It adds that "The QR code is not available at all Financial Institutions", and that the landing page may say "coming soon", in which case the customer should send by email or phone number ([Zelle FAQ, QR code](https://www.zelle.com/faq/how-do-i-use-zelle-qr-code)). **Verified:** nothing on zelle.com or any bank page documents the `qr-codes?data=` format or invites embedding it as a link. Sharing it is sanctioned, though: U.S. Bank's app shares the code "by text, email, copy" ([KB0212392](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0212392.html)).
- **Survives a bank without tags?** The page does not care about tags; it never reads the payload. Whether a given bank's scanner accepts a tag payload is up to that bank, and unverified.

## 3. Can the amount be preset?

**No.** Every route was checked. A customer can be put in front of a set amount only through a request the owner sends from the bank app, and only if the customer is already enrolled with Zelle.

### Presetting the amount: what each route gives

**(a) Extra fields in the QR payload. Observed: nothing on the web side. The bank apps are untested, and U.S. Bank says no.**
- I encoded three variants and opened each: `{"token":"expandhandyman","name":"EXPAND","action":"PAYMENT","amount":"2854.37","memo":"Invoice 1001"}`, `{…,"requestedAmount":285437,"amt":"2854.37","note":"Invoice 1001"}` and `{…,"action":"REQUEST","amount":"2854.37"}`. I also tried a non-base64 string and no `data` at all. The server returned the same page every time, 200, with only the echoed query string differing. In the browser the page showed no amount, memo or payee for the amount variant, and the U.S. Bank link simply carried the longer base64 along unchanged.
- The page's script treats `data` as opaque and never decodes it (§2). Only a bank that receives it (U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo, BofA and a few others) could act on an extra field. **Unverified**, since testing it needs a logged-in banking app. **Verified against it:** U.S. Bank, the bank that issued this code, says "you can't add an amount when using a QR code. The recipient chooses how much to send. If there's a specific amount you want, contact that person" ([KB0212392](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0212392.html)). U.S. Bank's own scan flow ends with the customer completing "Enter Amount" ([KB0213890](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0213890.html)), and Zelle's is "scan … enter the amount, hit Send" ([Zelle FAQ, QR code](https://www.zelle.com/faq/how-do-i-use-zelle-qr-code)).
- The payload's known fields are `token`, `name` and `action`. Wells Fargo describes the code's content the same way: "Your QR code includes your first or business name and either the email address, U.S mobile number, or Zelle tag" ([WF Zelle FAQs](https://www.wellsfargo.com/help/online-banking/zelle-faqs/)). No page describes any other field.

**(b) A Zelle request. Verified: it carries an amount and a memo, but only to an enrolled email or phone, and only from inside the bank app.**
- **Verified:** a request names the recipient's "email address or U.S. mobile number, the amount you'd like to send or request, review and add a memo" ([Zelle FAQ, how can I use Zelle](https://www.zelle.com/faq/how-can-i-use-zelle)). "Payment requests can only be sent to enrolled Zelle users" ([zelle.com/business](https://www.zelle.com/business), footnote 4; the same sentence is in [BofA's business Zelle page](https://promotions.bankofamerica.com/digitalbanking/mobilebanking/zelle/business)). Wells Fargo: "to send payment requests to a U.S. mobile number, the number must already be enrolled with Zelle" ([WF Zelle FAQs](https://www.wellsfargo.com/help/online-banking/zelle-faqs/)). U.S. Bank business accounts "can now receive and request Zelle payments" with a tag ([U.S. Bank, Mar 24 2026](https://www.usbank.com/about-us-bank/news-and-stories/article-library/customizing-the-zelle-experience-for-small-business-owners.html)).
- **What the customer gets:** a notification by email or text, "sent by Zelle or by their bank or credit union" ([Zelle FAQ, notified](https://www.zelle.com/faq/will-person-i-send-money-be-notified)), and the request in their banking app, where they "can choose to decline or ignore"; an ignored request "expires after 30 days" (U.S. Bank's rule, [KB0070118](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0070118.html)). The owner can cancel a pending request ([KB0069540](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0069540.html)). **Unverified:** the exact screen at each receiving bank, and whether paying it is one tap.
- **A caveat in the network terms (verified):** requesters agree they are not using the service "to request money for the payment or collection of an overdue or delinquent debt", and requests may be cut off if they seem "unwelcome by the recipient" (Standard Terms §12, [link](https://onlinelcsb.com/assets/files/rP5RQoXy)). A request for a freshly sent invoice seems fine. Requests as an Overdue reminder are not what the terms allow.
- **Verified: no request from outside the bank app.** Zelle offers no public API, and no link format for creating a request appears on zelle.com or any bank page I read. The bank APIs that mention Zelle are for sending money out, not requesting it: J.P. Morgan's "Initiate a Zelle Disbursement" ([J.P. Morgan developer docs](https://developer.payments.jpmorgan.com/docs/treasury/global-payments/capabilities/global-payments/zelle-disbursements/how-to/initiate-a-zelle-payment-request)) and U.S. Bank's "Disbursements via Zelle" ([U.S. Bank developer portal](https://developer.usbank.com/products/disbursements-via-zelle/v1), name only; the page did not render). So the app cannot send a request. The owner could send one by hand from the U.S. Bank app when he emails the invoice, to the customer's enrolled email or phone.

**(c) A bank deep link with an amount parameter. Unavailable, as far as public pages and the links themselves show.**
- No bank page I read documents a Zelle deep link, let alone an amount parameter (U.S. Bank, BofA, Chase, Wells Fargo, PNC). The only bank links in play are the ones Zelle's finder builds (§2), and none carries an amount; they carry only `context=qr-codes` and `data`. Wells Fargo's `p2pAmount` path is the closest thing, but it takes no amount parameter and, in a browser, goes to the app download page (**observed**).

**(d) Zelle small-business features that fix an amount. None found.**
- What Zelle offers a small business: receive, request, send, pay employees ([zelle.com/business](https://www.zelle.com/business)), plus a tag and a QR code. The fixed-amount features on bank pages are request and "Split the bill" (U.S. Bank [support](https://www.usbank.com/online-mobile-banking/zelle-person-to-person-payments/zelle-support.html); Chase "to request a specific amount of money … Request/Split with Zelle", [Chase business Zelle](https://www.chase.com/business/support/banking/online-banking/zelle)). Both are requests to an enrolled contact, covered in (b). Zelle offers no payment link, invoice or checkout for small businesses.

**So, for the paper and the link:** the customer types the amount. The paper should make that easy: Amount Due large and near the Zelle line, and the tag short enough to type. If the owner wants a preset amount for a customer, the only way is to send a request by hand from his bank app, and only to customers whose email or phone is already on Zelle.

## 4. Putting the QR on the paper

- **Observed: the encoded data is stable and simple.** `data` is standard base64 (with `=` padding) of the compact JSON `{"token":"expandhandyman","name":"EXPAND","action":"PAYMENT"}`, key order token, name, action. Re-encoding that exact string gives the bank's value byte for byte: `eyJ0b2tlbiI6ImV4cGFuZGhhbmR5bWFuIiwibmFtZSI6IkVYUEFORCIsImFjdGlvbiI6IlBBWU1FTlQifQ==`. There is no signature, timestamp, nonce or account reference. So drawing the QR with a library from a stored tag gives the same code the bank shows.
- **Unverified:** what the `name` field is for. The bank chose `EXPAND`, not "Expand Handyman LLC", and bank apps may show it or look the tag up in the Zelle directory; Zelle tells senders to check "the contact name that is displayed". The safest course is to copy the bank's exact string rather than invent the name. Two ways to do that: hold the whole URL the owner's code decodes to, or hold the tag and fix the name as `EXPAND`. Either way, scan the app-drawn code once with the owner's own bank app and one other bank's app before customers see it.
- **Unverified: the format is undocumented** (§2). Zelle could change it without notice. Drawing it ourselves means a later change breaks our code while the bank's image keeps working, and pasting the bank's image means a changed tag leaves a stale image. The bank's own share options are print, download, text and email ([KB0210290](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0210290.html), [KB0212392](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0212392.html)).
- **Verified: printing it on invoices is sanctioned.** Bank of America tells businesses to "save a PDF of your code to be used on invoices, emails or point of sale signage" and "Add QR codes to invoices to get paid fast", and to print the tag "along with your QR code … on invoices" ([BofA business Zelle](https://promotions.bankofamerica.com/digitalbanking/mobilebanking/zelle/business)). Nothing in the Standard Terms or on zelle.com restricts where a business shows its tag or code.
- **"If you unenroll, shared codes will be invalid."** I did not find this sentence on any public Zelle or bank page; it appears to be the bank's in-app text. It follows from the payload, though: the code is just the tag, so a code whose tag is unenrolled or changed points at nothing (or, once someone else takes the tag, at someone else; **unverified** whether Zelle ever releases tags for reuse). An app-drawn code updates everywhere as soon as the setting changes, which is the one real advantage over a pasted image.
- **Trademark (verified):** third-party fair use "can only apply to plain text … references to Zelle Marks … Fair Use does not extend to any Zelle logos or stylized trademarks" ([Zelle trademark guidelines](https://www.zelle.com/zelle-trademark-guidelines)). Print "Zelle®" as text, not the purple logo.
- **Where a QR helps.** A code on the paper is useful when the customer holds the paper or a laptop screen and pays from a phone, the flow Zelle designed. A code on the invoice link opened on the same phone is not useful (§2), because most customers would land on "scan in your banking app".

## 5. What the app should hold, and what the paper says

**Recommendation:** replace the Zelle email setting with a **Zelle tag** setting, validated to Zelle's rules (6 to 40 characters, letters, digits and hyphens, stored lowercase since tags are not case sensitive). Keep an email only if the owner confirms it is enrolled to the same account. With one field there are fewer concepts, and the tag is the thing the bank enrolled.

The QR payload is built from the tag with the name the bank uses (`EXPAND`), or the owner pastes the link his code decodes to. Either way, check it once against the bank's image (§4).

The invoice paper's How to pay block, in its current voice:

> **Zelle®:** send to the Zelle tag **expandhandyman** from your banking app, or scan the code with your banking app. Put invoice 1001 in the memo. The payment shows as EXPAND.
> [QR code]
> **Check:** payable to Expand Handyman LLC, handed to us in person.

- If an enrolled email is also set: "…send to the Zelle tag **expandhandyman** (or pay@expandhandyman.com if your bank does not take tags)…".
- "The payment shows as …" must say what the customer's bank actually shows. The current paper says "Expand Handyman LLC", while the QR payload says `EXPAND` and the owner's bank shows "expandhandyman". **Unverified:** what a paying customer sees. The owner can find out by sending $1 to the tag from a personal account, then write that name here.
- **The invoice link** shows the same block as text, with the tag copyable and the code visible for a second device. No "tap to pay with Zelle" button: tapping lands on the bank finder (§2).
- **The email** keeps its plain Zelle line; the QR code needs the paper or the link.

## 6. Helping the owner match a Zelle payment to an invoice

- **Verified:** Zelle's flow includes a memo ([Zelle FAQ](https://www.zelle.com/faq/how-can-i-use-zelle)). U.S. Bank puts the memo field under Amount "to help your recipient know what the funds are for" ([KB0069543](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0069543.html)) and shows received memos under Zelle Activity ([KB0206725](https://www.usbank.com/customer-service/knowledge-base/KB0206725.html)). PNC has "an optional memo" ([PNC](https://www.pnc.com/en/small-business/banking/zelle.html)), and BofA and Wells Fargo have a message or description field.
- **Unverified:** whether every sending bank passes the memo on to the recipient, and any length limit (none found).
- **What to ask of the customer:** "Put invoice 1001 in the memo." The invoice number alone is short and survives any memo limit. Customers will still forget, so the owner also matches by amount and sender name. The books effort owns that; the app does not watch the bank.

## What would change the map

- **The email on the paper may not work.** The owner enrolled the tag, not an email. The map's "Zelle and check stay on the paper as they are" keeps `pay@expandhandyman.com`, which may not be enrolled; if so, Zelle payments sent to it wait 14 days and bounce. The Zelle line should change to the tag, which departs from "as they are".
- **No preset amount, no tap-to-pay.** Zelle stays "the free way" without a one-tap path. Pay now through Stripe stays the only tap-to-pay route on the link.
- **A QR code on the paper** is a small new element in the How to pay block, drawn from the tag setting. It is optional: the tag as text does most of the work.
